Check the Integration of the Applications

Your group must be able to demonstrate at least two important shared processes.

Confirm that:

  • Flutter and ASP.NET connect to the same Supabase backend;
  • information entered through one application can be retrieved through the other, where required;
  • updates made through one application are reflected when the other retrieves the information;
  • both applications use consistent database fields and values; and
  • users can access only the functions and information permitted by their roles.
