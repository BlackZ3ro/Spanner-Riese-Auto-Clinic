# Zealots Tracker — Design Plan (for the group)

**What it is:** a shared, live board for Phase 2 — the 20 real tasks from
our checklist, who owns each one, who reviews it, and a running notes feed
so updates stay attached to the actual work instead of scattered across
WhatsApp. Link: **https://claude.ai/artifact/3TeRkof9dpEMUGjAiz2iDo**

## Why it looks the way it does

Built around Spanner/"The Zealots" rather than a generic to-do app look —
workshop signage type (condensed headings), a rust-orange accent pulled
from a torque wrench, steel-blue for secondary info, warm off-white/
graphite backgrounds. Status colours (green/amber/grey) are the only
"loud" colour beyond that — done on purpose, so a glance at the board
tells you what's actually blocked without reading every card.

## Layout

- **Top bar** — days left to 12 Oct, how many tasks are done vs in
  progress, and an overall progress bar.
- **Board** — one column per person (Wisani, Gary, Kea, Khumo, Denny,
  Ofentse & Breyton, Whole group), their tasks as cards. Click a card to
  see the reviewer, due date, which rubric line it feeds, and to move its
  status.
- **Updates feed** — below the board. Post a note against a specific task
  (pick it from the dropdown) or as a general update. Shows your real
  name and the time.

## How to use it

1. Open the link above.
2. Click your own cards as you start/finish something — move it to "In
   progress" when you pick it up, "Done" when it's actually done (not
   "basically done").
3. When something's worth telling the group — a blocker, a decision, a
   question — post it as an Update tied to the task, instead of a
   separate WhatsApp message. It stays attached to the work, so anyone
   catching up later sees the "why" right next to the "what."
4. Nobody edits someone else's card's ownership from here — if a task
   needs reassigning, that's a group decision, tell Wisani and she'll
   update it (and the official tracker) together.

**One real limitation, not a bug:** ticking things off and posting
updates only works while you're signed into Claude under the same account
group as Wisani — that's what makes it "live" for everyone. If you don't
have that, you can still open the link and read the board; ask Wisani to
log your update for you and she'll add it.

This board is a day-to-day view, not a submission document — it doesn't
replace the SRS, the Excel Progress Tracker, or GitHub. Its job is making
sure everyone always knows "what's left and who's on it" without another
meeting.
